# Histoire

L'histoire, racontée du point de vue de Saphira après sa fuite avec sa compagne, revisite toute la mythologie de l'époque de sa grand-mère.

À un moment donné, le royaume de Saidoras commença à décliner, principalement à cause d'une terrible sécheresse qui ravagea la région.
En conséquence, Eyaris offrit des terres et de la magie en échange de la main de la reine de l'époque.
(Elle gouverne la plupart du temps seule, car la reine d'Eyaris tomba malade.)

Création d'Eydoras (758), Guerre en 759

Car dans ce lieu, seules les femmes ont le droit de régner, et, étrangement, cela semble convenir à tous.

Pour moi, Saphira serait donc l'arrière-petite-fille de Gin, en quelque sorte.
Et elle serait donnée en mariage aux Alkidiens.

Les médecins sont tenus d'enregistrer chaque enfant né dans leur hôpital et de prouver cet enregistrement sous peine de trahison et de mort.
Pour les orphelins et les enfants abandonnés, c'est aux autorités de s'en occuper.

# Specialties

4 empires, based on the 4 types of game cards, etc.

Each empire has a specialty:

♥️ Specialized in agriculture and fertility
♦️ Specialized in culture, discovery, and knowledge
♠️ Specialized in technology and development (they are much more developed)
♣️ Specialized in weapons, etc.

Blue: Eyaris (♦️)
Yellow: Alkidia (♠️)
Green: Kaycan (♣️)
Red: Saidoras (♥️)

# Wealth
We're very focused on the monarchy of the four kingdoms, but in terms of wealth:
♠️ Alkidia: technological wealth, making them two of the most threatening in the territory
♥️ Saidoras: their focus on food makes them quite powerful, haha
♦️ Eyaris: they rely heavily on tourism and their culture, making this country a major hub
♣️ Kaycan: or rather, the garbage dump. They sometimes manage to scavenge a few small weapons and items from Alkidia, but basically everyone is separated from the other countries by a massive wall to keep the poor and crime away.

There was a time when the kingdom of Saidoras began to falter, mainly due to a severe drought that devastated everyone. As a result, Eyaris offered to give them land in exchange for the hand of the queen at the time. (who is therefore co-manager with the princess of Eyaris, but married to the prince, aka the older brother of the blue-haired girl)

Because in this place: only women have the right to rule lol, and strangely enough, everyone takes it well.

So for me, Saphira would be Gin's great-granddaughter, basically.
And she would be given in marriage to the Alkidians.
